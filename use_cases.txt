1. EXECUTIVE SYSTEM OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
ATLAS replaces manual paperwork, spreadsheets, and disconnected chat groups with
a single, unified university platform. The academic journey proceeds across five
formal phases:

  PHASE 1: Enrollment, Team Formation & Supervisor Matching
  PHASE 2: Proposal Drafting, Revision & Formal Departmental Approval
  PHASE 3: Milestone Progress Tracking, Code/Document Submissions & Feedback
  PHASE 4: Defense Committee Scheduling & Automated Conflict Prevention
  PHASE 5: Multi-Criteria Rubric Scoring, Final Grade Computation & Publishing

All users enter through the landing page (http://127.0.0.1:8000), log in via /login,
and are routed directly to role-specific dashboard views at /dashboard.


================================================================================
2. ACTOR 1: STUDENT (THESIS CANDIDATE) WORKFLOWS
================================================================================
A student is an undergraduate or postgraduate candidate completing a capstone
project or research thesis.

USE CASE S-01: Registration & Account Setup
--------------------------------------------
- Goal: Create an active student profile in ATLAS.
- Steps:
  1. Navigate to http://127.0.0.1:8000/register
  2. Fill in: Full Name, University Email (@atlas.edu or personal), Password.
  3. Note: Public registration automatically provisions the user as a STUDENT.
  4. Submit form. Upon success, redirect to /login.
  5. Log in with credentials to land on the Student Dashboard.

USE CASE S-02: Team Creation & Member Invites
----------------------------------------------
- Goal: Form a project team with peers within the active semester limits.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Teams > Create Team (/dashboard/teams/new)
- Steps:
  1. Team Leader clicks "+ Create Team".
  2. Enters:
     - Team Name (e.g., "Team Alpha")
     - Proposed Project / Thesis Title
     - Description / Research Objective
     - Selects the Active Academic Semester.
  3. Clicks "Create Team".
  4. System generates a unique 10-character Invite Code (e.g., "xK9_mQ2A").
  5. Leader copies the Invite Code and shares it with teammates.

USE CASE S-03: Teammate Joining a Team
---------------------------------------
- Goal: Join an existing team created by a peer.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Teams > Join Team (/dashboard/teams/join)
- Steps:
  1. Teammate logs into their student account.
  2. Enters the Team Invite Code.
  3. System validates:
     - User is not already in an active team for this semester.
     - Team has not exceeded the semester maximum member capacity (e.g., 4 members).
  4. Member is added to the team roster.

USE CASE S-04: Supervisor Discovery & Request
---------------------------------------------
- Goal: Pair the team with an eligible faculty supervisor.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Supervisors (/dashboard/supervisors)
- Steps:
  1. Browse faculty profiles, research areas, and current student capacity.
  2. Click "Request Supervision" on the selected faculty member.
  3. Fill in: Brief pitch, research motivation, proposed methodology.
  4. Submit request.
  5. Team status updates to "Supervisor Requested".
  6. Real-time notification is sent to the faculty member.

USE CASE S-05: Thesis Proposal Submission & Revision
-----------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Submit the formal thesis proposal for faculty approval.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Proposals (/dashboard/proposals)
- Steps:
  1. Once a supervisor is assigned, navigate to Proposal Management.
  2. Enter: Problem Statement, Research Objectives, Scope, Methodology.
  3. Upload Proposal Document (PDF).
  4. Click "Submit Proposal". Status becomes "Submitted / Under Review".
  5. If the supervisor requests changes, review specific revision notes.
  6. Edit and resubmit until status changes to "Approved".

USE CASE S-06: Milestone Deliverables & Code Repository Submission
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Submit deliverables for scheduled semester deadlines.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Submissions (/dashboard/submissions/new)
- Steps:
  1. Check upcoming milestone due dates on Dashboard or /dashboard/milestones.
  2. Click "New Submission" for the targeted milestone (e.g., "Midterm Prototype").
  3. Enter Submission Title and Description of progress made.
  4. Provide Deliverable Artifacts:
     - GitHub / GitLab Repository URL (e.g., https://github.com/atlas/health-monitor)
     - Uploaded Document / Report (PDF / DOCX)
     - Deliverable file path / archive link
  5. System validates deadline; if past due date, submission is flagged as "Late".
  6. Click "Submit". Supervisor is automatically notified.

USE CASE S-07: Reviewing Feedback & Scores
-------------------------------------------
- Goal: View supervisor comments, critique, and milestone evaluations.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Submissions > [Submission Details]
- Steps:
  1. Open the submission item.
  2. Read supervisor comments, revision directives, and milestone status.
  3. Click "Code Repository" to verify linked source code.
  4. If revision requested, click "Edit Submission", adjust deliverables, and resubmit.

USE CASE S-08: Defense Schedule & Defense Preparation
------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: View assigned oral presentation session, venue, and panel members.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Defense (/dashboard/defense)
- Steps:
  1. Check defense status: Scheduled date, time slot, and venue (or virtual room).
  2. View assigned Defense Committee / Panel Members.
  3. Download defense guidelines and prepare thesis presentation.

USE CASE S-09: Final Academic Grade & Evaluation Transcript
------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: View final published grades and rubric performance breakdown.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Evaluations (/dashboard/evaluations)
- Steps:
  1. Once defense is completed and coordinator publishes final grades:
  2. View overall grade (e.g., "A", score "92.5/100").
  3. View rubric score breakdown across criteria (Implementation, Report, Presentation).
  4. View supervisor final remarks and committee assessment.


================================================================================
3. ACTOR 2: SUPERVISOR (FACULTY MEMBER) WORKFLOWS
================================================================================
A supervisor is a faculty academic responsible for advising, mentoring, monitoring,
and grading assigned student teams throughout their thesis duration.

USE CASE V-01: Faculty Dashboard & Supervision Overview
--------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Monitor all supervised teams, pending reviews, and upcoming deadlines.
- Navigation: Dashboard (/dashboard)
- Steps:
  1. Log in as supervisor (e.g., supervisor1@atlas.edu).
  2. View quick metrics:
     - Number of supervised teams
     - Pending supervision requests
     - Submissions awaiting review
     - Upcoming defense committee assignments
  3. Access Quick Actions for grading and feedback.

USE CASE V-02: Reviewing & Accepting Supervision Requests
----------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Accept or decline student team mentorship requests.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Supervision Requests (/dashboard/supervisors/requests)
- Steps:
  1. View pending student requests with team details, project title, and proposal pitch.
  2. Check current student capacity (quota limit set by department).
  3. Choose action:
     - "Accept": Team status updates to "Supervisor Assigned", supervisor added to team.
     - "Decline": Provide constructive decline reason; team can request another faculty.

USE CASE V-03: Proposal Evaluation & Approval
----------------------------------------------
- Goal: Assess thesis proposal feasibility, scope, and technical depth.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Proposals (/dashboard/proposals)
- Steps:
  1. Open a team's submitted proposal.
  2. Read problem statement, objectives, and download proposal PDF.
  3. Actions:
     - "Request Revision": Add specific comments on methodology or scope.
     - "Approve": Proposal is locked and approved. Team moves to active execution.
     - "Reject": For unviable or duplicate proposals.

USE CASE V-04: Milestone Submission Review & Grading
-----------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Review deliverables, inspect GitHub code, and provide rubric grades.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Reviews / Submissions (/dashboard/submissions)
- Steps:
  1. View submissions submitted by supervised teams.
  2. Open submission details (e.g., /dashboard/submissions/{id}).
  3. Review student notes, download deliverable document, or click GitHub repository link.
  4. Select status:
     - "Under Review"
     - "Approved"
     - "Revision Required"
     - "Rejected"
  5. In the "Give Feedback" form:
     - Type detailed qualitative feedback.
     - Enter optional letter grade or mark (e.g., "A-", "88%").
  6. Submit Feedback. Student team receives an instant notification.

USE CASE V-05: Defense Committee Participation
-----------------------------------------------
- Goal: Attend scheduled student defenses and evaluate candidate presentations.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Defense (/dashboard/defense)
- Steps:
  1. View defense hearings where the supervisor is listed as panel member or chair.
  2. Inspect candidate team details, project title, and submitted final reports.
  3. Attend oral defense in designated room or link.

USE CASE V-06: Rubric-Based Final Evaluation
---------------------------------------------
- Goal: Score candidate teams against department-standard grading rubrics.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Evaluations (/dashboard/evaluations/new)
- Steps:
  1. Select the team being evaluated.
  2. Select the evaluation rubric (e.g., "Capstone Final Rubric 2026").
  3. Enter scores for each weighted criterion:
     - Technical Complexity & Rigor (e.g., 28 / 30)
     - System Implementation & Code Quality (e.g., 27 / 30)
     - Documentation & Thesis Report (e.g., 18 / 20)
     - Oral Presentation & Q&A Defense (e.g., 19 / 20)
  4. Total score is computed automatically.
  5. Add final supervisor commentary.
  6. Submit evaluation to department grade ledger.

4. ACTOR 3: COORDINATOR / ADMIN (DEPARTMENT LEAD) WORKFLOWS
================================================================================
The coordinator oversees departmental policies, semester setup, rubric matrices,
defense committee logistics, and final grade certification.

USE CASE C-01: Academic Semester Initialization & Governance
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Configure the active academic semester and lifecycle parameters.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Semesters (/dashboard/semesters)
- Steps:
  1. Click "+ New Semester".
  2. Enter:
     - Code: e.g., "SP2026", "FALL2026"
     - Name: e.g., "Spring 2026 Capstone Term"
     - Start Date & End Date
     - Max Team Size (e.g., 4) & Min Team Size (e.g., 2)
  3. Mark as "Active Semester".
  4. New student registrations and teams automatically bind to this active term.

USE CASE C-02: Rubric & Assessment Matrix Configuration
--------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Define standardized departmental rubrics with weighted criteria.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Rubrics (/dashboard/rubrics)
- Steps:
  1. Click "+ New Rubric".
  2. Name: e.g., "Final Capstone Evaluation Matrix".
  3. Add criteria items:
     - Criterion name, description, and maximum points (e.g., Problem Solving: 25).
  4. Set target total points (e.g., 100).
  5. Save and activate. Faculty members will grade using this standardized matrix.

USE CASE C-03: Milestone Deadlines & Timeline Calendar
-------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Establish deadlines across the semester for all teams.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Milestones (/dashboard/milestones)
- Steps:
  1. Click "+ New Milestone".
  2. Enter:
     - Title: "Proposal Submission", "Midterm Prototype", "Final Thesis Draft"
     - Description: Submission requirements, required code repo, report guidelines.
     - Due Date & Due Time
     - Associated Semester
  3. Publish milestone. Appears on all student and faculty calendars.

USE CASE C-04: Team Oversight & Faculty Workload Balancing
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Monitor team formation progress and balance supervisor loads.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Teams (/dashboard/teams)
- Steps:
  1. Filter teams by status (Forming, Supervisor Assigned, Proposal Approved, In Progress).
  2. Identify orphan teams that have not found a supervisor before deadline.
  3. Use Admin Reassignment to manually assign an available faculty supervisor.
  4. Ensure faculty supervision limits are balanced across the department.

USE CASE C-05: Defense Committee Scheduling & Automated Conflict Prevention
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Schedule thesis defense hearings without room or faculty double-bookings.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Defense (/dashboard/defense/new)
- Steps:
  1. Click "Schedule Defense".
  2. Select:
     - Target Candidate Team
     - Date & Time Slot (Start Time, End Time)
     - Venue / Room (e.g., "Lab 301", "Conference Hall B")
     - Panel Members: Select 2 or 3 faculty examiners.
  3. Click "Schedule Defense".
  4. Conflict Engine Verification:
     - If the room is already booked at that time -> Displays room collision warning.
     - If an assigned panelist is scheduled for another defense at that time ->
       Displays faculty double-booking collision error.
  5. Once validated, defense is confirmed and added to all participants' schedules.

USE CASE C-06: Grade Review, Calculation & Final Publishing
------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Compute aggregate weighted grades and publish final academic results.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Evaluations (/dashboard/evaluations)
- Steps:
  1. Review submitted evaluations from supervisors and defense committees.
  2. Verify scores against departmental grading distribution curve.
  3. Calculate final letter grade (A+, A, B+, B, etc.).
  4. Toggle "Publish Grades":
     - Unlocks official grades on student transcripts.
     - Marks teams as "Completed".

USE CASE C-07: Audit Logging & Academic Compliance
---------------------------------------------------
- Goal: Maintain an immutable ledger of system actions for accreditation.
- Navigation: Dashboard > Audit Logs (/dashboard/audit-logs)
- Steps:
  1. Inspect chronological activity log:
     - User authentications, role modifications
     - Proposal approvals, milestone uploads
     - Grade modifications and defense scheduling events
  2. Filter by user, date range, or action type for university compliance audits.

5. END-TO-END WALKTHROUGH SCENARIO ("TEAM ALPHA")
================================================================================
Here is how a real semester flows from start to finish in ATLAS:

1. COORDINATOR SETS UP THE SEMESTER:
   - Coordinator logs in (coordinator@atlas.edu).
   - Creates "Spring 2026" with max team size = 4.
   - Creates milestones: "Proposal" (Feb 15), "Midterm" (Mar 30), "Final" (May 10).
   - Creates grading rubric with 4 criteria totaling 100 points.

2. STUDENTS REGISTER & FORM TEAM:
   - Student Tahmid registers or logs in (e.g., student1@atlas.edu).
   - Tahmid creates "Team Alpha", titled "Smart Health Monitoring System".
   - System generates invite code: "K9Q_mZ81".
   - Teammates Samiul and Nahid register and use code "K9Q_mZ81" to join Team Alpha.

3. SUPERVISOR MATCHING:
   - Tahmid browses faculty at /dashboard/supervisors and selects Fatema Jannat Dihan.
   - Team Alpha submits a supervision request.
   - Supervisor Dihan logs in (supervisor1@atlas.edu), reviews the pitch, and accepts.

4. PROPOSAL APPROVAL:
   - Team Alpha writes their proposal and uploads "Proposal_v1.pdf" at /dashboard/proposals.
   - Supervisor Dihan reviews it, adds comments: "Refine sensor sampling section", requests revision.
   - Team Alpha updates text, re-uploads "Proposal_v2.pdf".
   - Supervisor Dihan approves. Team Alpha enters Phase 3 (Active Progress).

5. MILESTONE SUBMISSIONS & REVIEWS:
   - On March 28, Team Alpha completes their Midterm Prototype.
   - They submit at /dashboard/submissions:
     * Code URL: https://github.com/atlas-demo/health-monitor
     * File: Midterm_Deliverable.pdf
   - Supervisor Dihan accesses /dashboard/submissions, tests the repo, reviews code,
     and awards status "Approved" with grade "A" and feedback: "Clean architecture, good API docs."

6. DEFENSE SCHEDULING WITH CONFLICT AVOIDANCE:
   - Coordinator schedules Team Alpha defense for May 15 at 10:00 AM in Room 301.
   - Coordinator assigns Supervisor Dihan and Dr. Grace Hopper to the panel.
   - System checks room and panel availability; confirms zero scheduling conflicts.

7. DEFENSE & FINAL GRADING:
   - Team Alpha presents their oral defense in Room 301.
   - Supervisor Dihan and panel submit rubric evaluation: 94/100 points.
   - Coordinator reviews the final departmental ledger, assigns final grade "A".
   - Coordinator clicks "Publish Grades".
   - Tahmid, Samiul, and Nahid view their official completion transcript on their dashboard.
